> A note to myself — to think more clearly about what a good wife should be.
>
> P.S. I am HE (straight as an arrow). That does not excuse me from living up to the standard of a good husband, according to Paul's letter to the Ephesians — the husband's side of the coin:
>
> > "Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her."
> >
> > — Ephesians 5:25
>
> Keeping that in mind, I am writing this piece to myself. I may need to come back later to remind myself of what I should be doing. I think I will need this more than once.

Some set of questions which I asked myself was, "Why is the last chapter about woman ?" and also "This is an advice coming from a mother to a son who at the time was a king ?". Like why would a mother give advice to her son ?

Then I thought of the context of the chapter and the time it was written. The chapter was written in the 10th century BC, and the time it was written was during the reign of King Solomon (who married many women and had many children).One would have thought that he probably took he advice of his mother but here he was giving out a free advice.

Do all mothers give advice to their sons ? Maybe , Maybe not. But in this case, let me think about it as if I was given this advice by my mum 🤔.

Proverb 31:3 talks about not giving one's strength to women. So how can I give my strength to a woman ? By the way what is my strength as a man ? Is it physical, physcological, spiritual, emotional, etc contraint ?. As a man, I would have said probably physical and psychological but again it is more than that because if that was the case Samson would have not fallen for Delilah.
Then it goes further to say nor things or ways that destroys Kings and Princes. So what are these things or ways that destroys Kings and Princes ?

- Lust - Pornography, Sexuality, etc 
- Pride / Ego
- Drunkiness / Alcoholism

 > An assumption that King and Princes in this context can be replaced with the Word "Men"

Anyway, a detour here, but it is a good one. Proverbs 31:4-5 talks about Wine perhaps an observation made about the people in the 
court who become forgetful and lose control and judgement when drunk. Maybe ... just maybe Wine is a metaphor for some from of addiction.It goes further to say to kill a person, give him a strong drink. If by subsitution method, I replace the word "drink" with "addiction", it makes more sense. It implies that addiction can be a killer so regardless of what was used in the chapter, Addiction is designed to kill (I mean the bad kind of addiction). It is designed to temporarily shutdown one's misery (perhaps loneliness)

Let's get back to the main topic. Proverbs 31:10-11 talks about a virtous woman is priceless, trustworthy (so need for insecurity on the part of the husband) and deserving of praise. She is out for his best interest and not to do him evil. Likewise, the husband must lay down is life (an ultimate sacrifice) for his wife. Therefore, his confidence is boosted just by the thought of her just the way adrelaline does to a person.

Listed attributes of a virtuous woman are:
- She seeks wool and flax, and works with willing hands.
- She is like the merchant ships, she brings her food from afar.
- She rises while it is yet night, and provides food for her household, and portions for her maidens.
- She considers a field and buys it; with the fruit of her hands she plants a vineyard.
- She girds herself with strength, and makes her arms strong.
- She is vigorous and of good courage; her husband has confidence in her, and lacks nothing of value.
- She brings him good, and not evil, all the days of her life.

All this maps to the following: 
 - An investor
 - A salesperson
 - Fast thinker
 - Self sacrificing
 - Hard worker


Overall, she is kind, not lazy, loving, caring, supportive, and a good listener. (Proverbs 31:26)

At the end of the day, I will call her blessed and so will the kids, grandkids and great grandkids. Proverbs 31:28 says "Her children rise up and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praises her.


> From the above, I have my work cut out for me. I need to be a good husband and a good father. I need to be a good investor, salesperson, fast thinker, self sacrificing, hard worker, kind, not lazy, loving, caring, supportive, and a good listener.

This does not make me weak but rather strong. It makes me a better person and a better husband and father.

I still have questions but this is a good start.
