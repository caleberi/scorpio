I was approached by an acquaintance who is a Zig enthusiast yesterday and he mentioned that he was looking for a better community to grow and learn Zig because the current community is not organized and without order, which I agree with since I have just left both the Rust and Zig groups because of the indiscipline and disorderliness. So here I am writing this post to share my thoughts on how we can build a better community or at least what I think can be done to improve how a community can run and grow. This is not particularly about the Zig community but more of a template for me on community building and growth.

## Observations from the best communities

Over the years, I have been part of a few software engineering communities and I have been lucky enough to notice some patterns that help them grow fast and better. I think I might as well just highlight some of them here before drafting out a plan on how to build a Zig version for Nigeria. Here are some of them:

- **Clear Purpose and Vision:** Successful communities have a clear purpose and vision that guides their activities and interactions. This helps members understand the goals of the community and how they can contribute.

- **Constructive and Restricted Active Engagement:** Members are actively engaged in discussions that follow the rules of the community, events, and activities. This can include regular meetups, online forums, and collaborative projects.

- **Diverse and Inclusive Membership:** A thriving community welcomes members from diverse backgrounds and experiences. This diversity can lead to richer discussions, innovative ideas, and a broader range of perspectives.

- **Mentorship and Support:** Experienced members provide mentorship and support to newcomers, helping them navigate the community and develop their skills. This can include pairing new members with mentors, offering guidance on projects, and providing resources for learning.

- **Opportunities for Collaboration:** Communities that encourage collaboration among members foster a sense of belonging and shared purpose. This can include collaborative coding projects, hackathons, and joint research initiatives.

- **Decisive Leadership and Governance:** Effective leadership and governance structures help maintain order and ensure that the community operates smoothly. This can include clear roles and responsibilities, decision-making processes, and conflict resolution mechanisms. Any rule that is not followed should be enforced and the community should be able to take action against any member who is not following the rules. Think of this as the clubs you see in movies, e.g. golf clubs 😅

- **Book Reading and Paper Discussion:** Communities that engage in book reading and paper discussions provide members with opportunities to learn from experts, stay updated on the latest research, and deepen their understanding of key concepts. This can include book clubs, journal clubs, and discussion groups focused on specific topics.

- **Job Opportunities and Career Development:** When job opportunities show up a lot in a community, it will attract more members to join and also help the existing members to grow in their career. This can include job boards, career fairs, and networking events that connect members with potential employers and collaborators.

## Observations from the bad ones

Like I said earlier, I left the existing Rust and Zig communities because though they are very good, they tend to be rather intimidating, condescending, and unwelcoming to new members. I have seen some of the members in these communities be quite rude and disrespectful to new members who are trying to learn and grow. This is not a good way to build a community and it is not sustainable in the long run. Also they post things that are quite distracting and not related to the community purpose. Lastly, they don't have a moderator like a typical Reddit community or a Discord server that can enforce the rules and ensure that the community is running smoothly. With this, let me highlight some of the things that I think are not good for a community and should be avoided:

- **Unhelpful and Distracting Content:** Posting content that is not relevant to the community's purpose or that distracts from the main discussions can hinder the community's growth and effectiveness.

- **Intimidating and Unwelcoming Behavior:** Members who are condescending, rude, or dismissive towards newcomers can create a hostile environment that discourages participation and learning.

- **Lack of Moderation and Enforcement:** Without active moderation and enforcement of community rules, discussions can become chaotic, and members may feel unsafe or unsupported. This can lead to a decline in engagement and participation.

- **Lack of Clear Guidelines and Expectations:** Communities that do not have clear guidelines and expectations for behavior and participation can lead to confusion and frustration among members. This can result in misunderstandings, conflicts, and a lack of accountability.

I think that about covers it for now. I might have all I need to draft the Zig community plan for Nigeria. I hope you found this post helpful and insightful. If you have any thoughts or suggestions on how to build a better community, please feel free to share them in the comments below.

> PS - This is not my first time trying to build a community; I failed a couple of times before but I am not giving up. I will keep trying until I succeed. I believe that with the right approach and mindset, we can build a thriving and supportive community for Zig enthusiasts in Nigeria.
