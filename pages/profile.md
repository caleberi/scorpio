Glad you could check out this profile. As an engineer with a passion for building highly scalable and available applications, I work across multiple languages — including JavaScript, Python, C/C++, Go, TypeScript, and Java. Most of my production experience has been in JavaScript: shipping fintech applications, maintaining legacy systems, and improving day-to-day productivity for the teams that own them.

In this post I’ll share a short overview of how I work, the tools I reach for most often, and where you can find more of my writing and code.

### How I work

I care about systems that stay understandable under load: clear boundaries between services, durable data models, and tooling that makes failures easy to investigate. In fintech that usually means careful money movement, strong audit trails, and APIs that other engineers can trust without reading every line of the implementation.

The same habits show up in personal projects — packing content into addressable chunks, serving it from a small Zig API, and keeping the frontend focused on reading that source of truth.
